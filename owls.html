OVERVIEW

THE SYSTEM

A desktop optical weather display designed to transform live meteorological data into an atmospheric physical interface.

The system combines a concealed LCD, semi-reflective beamsplitter, Raspberry Pi, and procedural graphics to create the appearance of weather floating inside a physical enclosure.

01
CONCEPT

FROM SCREEN
TO OBJECT

The project began with a question: how can weather information exist as something physical rather than another conventional screen interface?

CONCEPT SKETCH / IMAGE

Early concept development / optical layout.

02
RESEARCH

OPTICAL
GEOMETRY

A teleprompter-style Pepper's Ghost configuration was selected to create the floating-image effect.

The display sits concealed in the base and faces upward toward a semi-reflective beamsplitter positioned at approximately 45 degrees.
